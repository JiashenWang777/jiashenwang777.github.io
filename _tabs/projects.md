Welcome to my IC Design Portfolio!

I am an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer
Engineering at the University of Florida,
focusing on analog and mixed-signal IC design.

This page documents my circuit design projects,
simulations, verification results, and lessons
learned throughout the engineering process.

## 1. Floating Inverter Amplifier (FIA)

**Status:** In Progress

**Focus:** Analog Circuit Design

This project explores the operating principles,
circuit design, and performance of the
Floating Inverter Amplifier.

### Planned Work
- Study FIA operating principles
- Develop transistor-level schematics
- Perform circuit simulations
- Analyze gain, noise, and power consumption
- Investigate design trade-offs

## 2. Pipeline ADC

**Status:** Planned

**Focus:** Mixed-Signal IC Design

This project aims to develop and verify a
pipeline analog-to-digital converter.

### Planned Work
- Study pipeline ADC architecture
- Design comparator and residue amplifier
- Analyze sampling and clock timing
- Integrate multiple ADC stages
- Evaluate SNDR, SFDR, and ENOB

## 3. Fully Differential Two-Stage Op-Amp

**Status:** Completed Course Project

**Tools:** Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre

Designed a two-stage fully differential
operational amplifier with common-mode
feedback (CMFB).

### Design Topics
- Transistor sizing and biasing
- Frequency compensation
- AC and transient simulations
- Stability and phase-margin analysis
- Layout and physical verification

## Engineering Philosophy

I believe that circuit design is best learned
through a continuous cycle of:

**Learn → Design → Simulate → Analyze → Improve**

I document not only successful results,
but also debugging experiences and
design iterations.
